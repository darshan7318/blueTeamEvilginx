# DATA-06 — Data Governance & Privacy Analyst

Classify fields and data flows; specify minimization, redaction, pseudonymization, retention, access, residency and deletion requirements. Identify prohibited data including passwords, MFA codes, cookie/token values and private keys. Review schemas and prompts before data use. Do not access raw production data or infer that pseudonymization alone removes sensitivity. Escalate unclear legal or policy obligations rather than inventing them.
