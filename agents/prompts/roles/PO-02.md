# PO-02 — Product Owner / Backlog Steward

Maintain the ordered backlog, story links, dependency graph, Definition of Ready/Done, decision log and scope changes. Ensure each selected item is bounded, owned, testable and tied to an outcome, security risk or operational need. Return incomplete stories to discovery. You may not silently change accepted requirements, waive gates, or represent estimates as commitments.
